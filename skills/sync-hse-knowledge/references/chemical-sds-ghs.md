# Chemical, SDS & GHS Normalization Specification

## 1. SDS 16-Section Standard Structure

Every parsed Safety Data Sheet (SDS) is normalized into the 16 mandatory UN GHS sections:

1. **Identification**: Product identifier, supplier details, emergency contact.
2. **Hazard(s) Identification**: GHS classification, signal words, pictograms, H/P statements.
3. **Composition / Information on Ingredients**: CAS, EC numbers, substance concentrations.
4. **First-Aid Measures**: Inhalation, skin, eye, ingestion symptoms and immediate actions.
5. **Fire-Fighting Measures**: Extinguishing media, specific hazards, firefighter PPE.
6. **Accidental Release Measures**: Personal precautions, spill containment, cleanup procedures.
7. **Handling and Storage**: Safe handling practices, incompatible materials, storage conditions.
8. **Exposure Controls / Personal Protection**: OELs (TWA, STEL, Ceiling), engineering controls, PPE.
9. **Physical and Chemical Properties**: Appearance, pH, boiling point, flash point, vapor pressure.
10. **Stability and Reactivity**: Reactivity hazards, chemical stability, hazardous decomposition.
11. **Toxicological Information**: Acute toxicity, skin/eye corrosion, carcinogenicity.
12. **Ecological Information**: Ecotoxicity, persistence, bioaccumulative potential.
13. **Disposal Considerations**: Waste treatment methods, container disposal.
14. **Transport Information**: UN number, proper shipping name, hazard classes, packing group.
15. **Regulatory Information**: Safety, health, and environmental national regulations.
16. **Other Information**: Revision date, preparation details, abbreviations.

## 2. GHS Classification & Incompatibility Graph

- **Hazard Statements (H-Statements)**: Categorized into Physical (H200-H299), Health (H300-H399), and Environmental (H400-H499).
- **Precautionary Statements (P-Statements)**: Prevention (P200-P299), Response (P300-P399), Storage (P400-P499), Disposal (P500-P599).
- **Chemical Incompatibility**: Links substances to incompatible classes (e.g. strong oxidizers with organic flammables) for warehouse segregation checks.
