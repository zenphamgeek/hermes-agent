# License, Entitlement & Copyright Protection Policy

## 1. Default-Deny Licensing Principle

Sources containing proprietary or copyrighted material (e.g. ISO 14001, ISO 45001, TCVN full texts, NFPA, ACGIH TLV) are operated under **`PENDING_ENTITLEMENT`** or **`LICENSE_REQUIRED`** mode until formal commercial licensing is uploaded.

## 2. Policy States

- `PUBLIC_DOWNLOAD`: Free public official government portals; raw bytes, parsed text, and vector embeddings stored.
- `PUBLIC_METADATA_ONLY`: Public metadata, clause numbers, and links stored; full text omitted.
- `LICENSE_REQUIRED`: Full content download blocked; pending license registration.
- `LICENSED`: Formal entitlement granted; full content parsed according to license terms.
- `INTERNAL_AUTHORIZED`: Internal enterprise documents (SOPs, site permits, SDS).
