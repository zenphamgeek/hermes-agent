# Hazard, Bow-Tie & Control Taxonomy Specification

## 1. Hierarchy of Controls

Controls are classified and prioritized according to the universal safety hierarchy:

1. **Elimination**: Physically remove the hazard (e.g. eliminate hazardous process step).
2. **Substitution**: Replace the hazard with a safer alternative (e.g. water-based solvent instead of toluene).
3. **Engineering Controls**: Isolate people from the hazard (e.g. local exhaust ventilation, interlocks, machine guarding).
4. **Administrative Controls**: Change the way people work (e.g. SOPs, Permit-To-Work, safety signage, work rotation).
5. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: Protect worker with equipment (e.g. chemical respirators, safety glasses).

---

## 2. Bow-Tie Modeling

- **Threats / Causes**: Initiating events (e.g. overpressure, corrosion, operator error).
- **Preventive Barriers**: Controls placed before top event.
- **Top Event**: Loss of containment or primary containment failure.
- **Mitigative Barriers**: Controls placed after top event to reduce consequence severity (e.g. deluge systems, bund walls).
- **Consequences**: Harm to people, environment, or asset.
