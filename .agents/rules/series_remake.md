# Series remake rules

These rules apply to every remake program, beginning with Series 1.

## Planner-first gate

`SERIES_CONTRACT_V1.json` and its hash-bound human approval are required before
any production mutation. The planner contains a unique claim graph and each
episode’s promise, prerequisite IDs, reinforcement, misconception, visual spine,
payoff, open loop and CTA target. The episode queue is topological, never
assumed from episode number.

## Remake quality rules

- Audit existing assets as evidence, not proof that a remake is approved.
- Use one canonical source for duration and information-load thresholds.
- Preserve useful existing components only when their math oracle, rendering and
  layout evidence remain current.
- Final audio precedes animation timing. Alignment must be recognized or
  manually corrected, never interpolated.
- Review a pilot scene before scaling a visual language across the series.
- Human approvals apply to the exact audio/media/dependency hash only.
- CTA is an instructional edge: payoff → unresolved question → valid successor.
  Do not point to a numerically next episode without a knowledge dependency.
- Shorts are 45–60 seconds, one proof and one Related Video target.

## S1 conflict resolutions

- Viewer profile is 22–40.
- Roboto Regular is the default Vietnamese display face.
- Voice is one continuous Vietnamese grammatical sentence with inline English
  terms, not bilingual voice splicing or a duplicate English sentence.
- No generic 5.8-second opener or static CTA clip interrupts the hook. Any
  opening/closing scene needs a narrative role in its Episode Contract.
- A declared reading hold must have a comprehension purpose; it does not exempt
  a scene from visual review.
